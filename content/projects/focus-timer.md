A Pomodoro-style focus timer that lives in the macOS menu bar: the remaining time shows in the menu bar, with a small popover for start, pause and reset. Focus and break cycles, sound and system notifications, day, week and all-time focus stats, adjustable durations and optional launch at login. Built with SwiftUI's MenuBarExtra, Combine, UserNotifications and ServiceManagement, with settings and stats stored in UserDefaults.
